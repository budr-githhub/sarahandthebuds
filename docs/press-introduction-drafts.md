## Sarah & the Buds — full band

Sarah & the Buds bring familiar songs and a welcoming, sing-along spirit to venues and celebrations around Central Kentucky. Their full-band sets draw from rock, country, Americana, folk and pop, with a wide-ranging songbook and room for requests. From wineries and porch concerts to festivals and private parties, they love bringing people together through songs worth sharing.

## Sarah & Bud — acoustic duo

Sarah & Bud offer an acoustic take on the songs you know and love. Playing around Central Kentucky, the duo draws from rock, country, Americana, folk and pop for intimate performances at wineries, restaurants, porch concerts and private celebrations. Their broad songbook leaves room for familiar favorites, a few discoveries and requests from the room.
