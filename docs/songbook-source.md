# Current songbook source

Released in PR #5 (`b99f8f1`). Public metadata: `src/data/songbook.json`. Homepage: `src/pages/index.astro`. Full reference: `src/pages/songbook.astro`.

Source is Bud’s August 2026 master DOCX, retained outside GitHub in `Documents/Plan & Adapt - Source Files`, plus Bud’s explicit additions: With or Without You, One, Acrobat, Falling Slowly and Song of Good Hope. Into the Mystic was already in the reference and is now also a Van Morrison homepage example.

Public reference contains 142 covers and four holiday selections (146). Originals are by request without titles; specialty songs are removed. Private source document remains unchanged. Only titles/artist metadata are published, never lyrics, chords, performance notes or suggested future additions. Duplicate master entries for Song for Someone and Emmylou were consolidated. The full reference now pairs every title with an artist/recording label. Artist names primarily follow the master table of contents and the approved homepage samples; cover versions are intentionally retained (for example, Joan Jett for Crimson and Clover and The Cranberries for Go Your Own Way). The title arrays are now records with `title` and `artist` fields. The homepage sample structure is unchanged.

Homepage has 44 artist rows in one native details/summary disclosure. Each row shows two examples or three short titles; rows wrap naturally. No individual artist disclosures. Full-list links appear above and below the section. Each section has a back-to-top link.

Earlier totals and named-original lists in the decision history are superseded by this public inventory.

## Artist labels — October 16, 2026

146 selections have artist labels. These describe familiar recordings, not exhaustive songwriting credits or a guarantee that the band copies one arrangement. Originals remain unnamed and by request. Holiday standard Have Yourself a Merry Little Christmas uses original recording artist Judy Garland; the master does not specify an arrangement. The master title A Very Willie Christmas is retained; its text identifies A Willie Nice Christmas by Kacey Musgraves featuring Willie Nelson ([official recording](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oGMTjNHKOng)).

Ambiguous titles were checked against the private song sections: Bruises matches Train featuring Ashley Monroe; When You’re Gone matches Bryan Adams and Melanie C; I Only Miss You matches Megan Moroney featuring Ed Sheeran ([recording](https://music.apple.com/us/song/1880181817)). Be Not So Fearful uses Bill Fay, the original artist, without asserting a particular cover arrangement. Full-list labels distinguish Glen Hansard’s Song of Good Hope, The Swell Season’s Low Rising, and separate Elle King / Tyler Childers recordings of Jersey Giant; homepage artist groupings are retained. No lyrics, chords or private performance notes are included in the repository.
